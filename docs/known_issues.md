# Known issues

- Source values are reused under the Licence Nationale de Données Publiques Ouvertes, the default for ONAGRI open data; the bulletin page itself does not state a licence. Bulletins are not redistributed except two samples. The catchment and natural-network layers inherit non-commercial terms from FABDEM and MERIT Hydro (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0); see `LICENSE-DATA`.
- Hydrographic basin remains unavailable for 30 dams. Six Medjerda assignments use explicit official basin-context evidence; no remaining basin was inferred from coordinates or bulletin region.
- Arabic labels exist for only part of the 36-dam roster. El Hma, Bou Heurtma, and Ziatine mappings retain candidate/qualified evidence status.
- Page 2 printed capacity contains unresolved transient or alternating episodes for Brek, Lakhmess, Kebir, Moula, and Rmel. `capacity_history.csv` admits durable regimes and exposes the exclusions in notes.
- Page 2 previous-hydraulic-year columns (`data/bulletin_page2.csv`) on the four bulletins of 2021-01-14 to 2021-01-17 differ from the values printed before and after for 32 dams (e.g. Sidi Salem level 106.31 m instead of 107.31 m). They are kept as printed.
- July 2022: on the bulletins of 2 and 3 July 2022 all 36 dams repeat the previous day's storage on both Page 1 and Page 2, while Page 3 keeps changing; storage then drops by 10.728 Mm³ on 4 July (`qa/INTERPAGE_MISMATCH_REVIEW/`). Whether the bulletin was stale or copied in error is unresolved; daily changes on 2–4 July should not be used as physical balance evidence.
- Page 2 *R.S. (g/l)* has no printed legend, so `p2_rs_unresolved_prohibited` asserts no meaning. Mellègue's second Page 2 line (*Cote PHE*) is not extracted. For Brek, Sficifa, Rmil and Sidi Aïch, similar printed levels go with very different volumes in different years; the cause is not established.
- Capacity metadata comes from durable printed Page 2 observations. It is not independent engineering confirmation of physical capacity or commissioning dates.
- Bulletin URLs are established for 3,979 of 3,985 dates (`sources/original_url_evidence.csv`); 1,609 rely on the listing date plus server filename rather than a page-hash chain, and nine have an ONAGRI listing title whose date differs from the bulletin's own date. Six dates (2019-09-28, 2020-06-01, 2022-07-08, 2024-08-24 to 2024-08-26) have no matching listing entry. The logical manifest contains 23 partial page sets, two Page 3 filename-date disagreements, and 3,270 physical-file paths without a supported canonical date. The complete physical inventory remains in `sources/file_manifest.csv`.
- Page 3 scientific authority remains PROBABLE in the frozen reconstruction.
- Bouhertma→Bou Heurtma and Zayatin→Ziatine Page 3 node crosswalks remain candidate mappings. E2 leaves the corresponding stable dam endpoint blank and retains the Page 3 node ID.
- The selected Page 3 transfer product records `SAME_DAY`, while its canonical-base contract describes minus-one-calendar-day semantics for transfers. E2 preserves the product field and discloses the discrepancy; it does not reconcile it.
- Page 3 source-consistency status is `NOT_CHECKED` for the transfer facts used here. Sejnane source-label regime limitations remain row flags.
- Capacity is unavailable for the explicit Brek, Lakhmess, and Mellègue gaps listed in `provenance/CAPACITY_UNKNOWN_PERIODS.csv`; lookup does not forward-fill them.
- Storage above useful capacity is a diagnostic, not an error (KPI recompute, 2026-09-30, `qa/kpi/`). "The capacity used is the printed *useful capacity at the last survey*. Storage above it is physically possible, for example in flood surcharge or after sedimentation surveys." Of the 1,488 rows above capacity (1.15 %, diagnostic D1), 836 have a printed fill and all 836 print fill ≥ 100 % in the bulletin itself. 149 rows have unavailable capacity. `qa/physical_validity.csv` still applies the earlier rule that counted these rows as failures.
- Printed fill agrees with storage/capacity for 84,470 of 84,471 eligible rows once the rounding of storage (0.001 Mm³) and capacity (0.01 Mm³) is included (KPI K11). "The only real mismatch is Masri, 9 February 2022: printed 44.9 %, computed 43.87 %." The earlier figure of 3,657 mismatches in `qa/fill_consistency.csv` comes from a tolerance that ignored those two roundings. 44,770 storage rows have no independently printed fill value.
- Source error: "The Ziatine and Gamgoum season-to-date inflows are printed swapped from 2017 to 2021 (checked in `13-1-2018_1.pdf`)." The extraction is faithful; the published values are not swapped back.
- Month-to-date and season-to-date counters on bulletin D cover day D−1: they reset on the bulletin of the 2nd of the month and of 2 September. QA that resets on the bulletin date (the earlier `DAM_QA_SUBMISSION_CLEAN` package) misclassifies every reset day.
- `transfers.csv` stores `date = bulletin_date` with `effective_date_rule = SAME_DAY` for all 47,556 rows. DLO 0.3 verified that `today` is the amount for day D−1 (`DAY_BEFORE_BULLETIN`) and `current_season_to_date` is an interval (`SEASON_TO_DATE`). A schema proposal adding explicit time columns is in `qa/kpi/schema/transfers_contract_v2_proposal.json`; it is not applied.
- "Mellègue has no canonical accepted total and is absent." "Sidi Salem has an accepted total but no final incremental geometry because its ordinary incremental/network status depends on the unresolved Mellègue system." The Sidi Saad and Sidi Aïch incremental geometries "retain special/upstream-blocker status and must not be treated as ordinary model-ready domains." `natural_network.geojson` "is not a complete river network." (`docs/gis.md`)
- E6A completeness uses the governed 36-dam roster for every accepted Page 1 bulletin date. Historical roster expansion therefore appears as explicit leading missingness, most strongly for Sarrat, rather than silently redefining the denominator.
- E6A temporal screening uses an analyst threshold of absolute robust z >= 6.0. MAD-zero groups and long-gap pairs remain excluded or reviewable; outliers are not automatic scientific errors. 7,644 daily changes get no score because the per-dam MAD is 0, so the 4.44 % flag rate is computed on a reduced population and is reported as a diagnostic, not a quality KPI.
- E6A.2 source localization inherits production lineage bounding boxes. Values are reread without calling production parser functions, but the audit is computer-assisted and is not a fully independent human/manual extraction audit.
- Eight Page 3 primary cells were unverifiable from the preserved source-cell evidence and are excluded from the A3 denominator. Page 3 remains `PROBABLE`; A3 transcription results do not establish semantic correctness.
- A3B retains unresolved stable-ID mappings and effective-date semantics as categorical dispositions. It is not combined with Page 3 transcription accuracy.
- Two complete Page 1 dates disagree with the independently printed national total beyond the precision-derived rounding bound. Four additional 2015-10-03 through 2015-10-06 dates are evidence-backed `SOURCE_ROSTER_SCOPE_DIFFERENCE` exclusions, and fourteen canonical dates are excluded for missing/incomplete Page 1 total evidence.
- No Page 3 daily row meets every direct-transfer comparison requirement. This is an explicit evidence limitation, not a statement that transfers failed to balance.
- This repository packages accepted outputs. It is not a standalone rerun of the scientific extraction systems. Rebuilding E2 needs inputs that are not in the repository (the V010 review package and the full reconstruction, see `config/paths.example.toml`).
- Paths to inputs outside the repository are written with logical root tokens such as `<FULL_RECONSTRUCTION>` or `<BULLETIN_CORPUS>`; their meaning is listed in `config/paths.example.toml`. SHA-256 values next to them identify the exact files.
